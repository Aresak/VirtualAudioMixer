Routing matrix

Every input against every bus. The strip buttons cover the fast path; this is the whole picture, and it is where a wiring mistake is actually visible.

Online is a mix-minus bus (D4). Its own source is excluded automatically and shown as N-1 — it cannot be switched on by hand. Hearing your own voice back with 200 ms of delay makes a meeting unusable, and it is the easiest mistake to make.

Automix

Gain sharing, not gating. Each channel's gain is its own level divided by the sum of all levels, smoothed — so nothing ever audibly opens or closes.

Never mandatory. The global switch lives in the status bar, where it is one click from any view if the automixer misbehaves mid-session — and where it turns amber while it is off, so nobody forgets. Individual channels opt out under Participate in gain sharing in the channel panel; the audience microphone and the online feed are out by default.
